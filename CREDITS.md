# Credits and licenses

This branch has **no Wolfenstein 3D material** (id Software): no textures,
sprites, status bar, digits, sounds, music, title art, names or level names
from that game. Everything that replaced it was made for this project.

## Original, made for this project (MIT, like the code)

Generated by code in `scripts/oms_art` (no third-party images or recordings
are used as input). Re-create with `make assets`.

| Files | What | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/oms3d/textures/*.bmp` | walls, doors, lift, lever (64×64, plus `-dark` variants) | `gen.py`: procedural stone, brick, wood, rock, metal; own emblem (gold star in a ring) |
| `assets/oms3d/textures/billboards/sentinel/` | enemy "Sentinela" (patrol robot), 8 directions, walk, aim, shoot, alert, hit, 5 death frames | `vox.py` + `characters.py`: voxel models rendered to pixel art |
| `assets/oms3d/textures/billboards/hound/` | enemy "Cão-Robô" (robot hound), 8 directions, run, bite, hit, death | same |
| `assets/oms3d/textures/billboards/*.bmp` | props and pickups (barrels, plants, lamps, tables, statue, banner, medkit, gem, trophy, treasure, …) | `decor.py` |
| `assets/oms3d/textures/items/{blaster,rifle,cannon,blade}/` | first-person weapons and HUD icons | `decor.py` |
| `assets/oms3d/textures/hud/stats/` | status bar with the robot-monitor face (7 health states × 3 looks, dead, cheat) | `gen.py` |
| `assets/fonts/hud/` | status bar digits and `%` | `gen.py` (7-segment style) |
| `assets/textures/missao.bmp` | hub poster for the campaign | `gen.py` |
| `assets/textures/oms-wall.bmp`, `assets/textures/pv-wall.bmp` | wood wall with a hanging banner | `gen.py`; the coat of arms comes from `assets/textures/oms.bmp` and the portrait (`scripts/oms_art/src/pv-portrait.png`) from the authors' own art |
| `assets/pt/textures/portugal.bmp` | stone wall with a banner and a simplified Portuguese shield | `gen.py` (the national arms are public domain) |
| `assets/oms3d/sounds/*.mp3` | weapon, pickup, door, robot, alert sounds and 4 music loops (`music-1`, `music-2`, `music-3`, `music-mega`) | `sounds.py`: synthesized (square/triangle/saw waves and noise); melodies written by the script |

`assets/textures/oms-text`, `assets/textures/oms.bmp`, `assets/textures/42.bmp`,
