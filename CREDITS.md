# Credits and licenses

This branch has **no Wolfenstein 3D material** (id Software): no textures,
sprites, status bar, digits, sounds, music, title art, names or level names
from that game. Everything that replaced it was made for this project.

## Original, made for this project (MIT, like the code)

Generated by code in `scripts/oms_art` (no third-party images or recordings
are used as input). Re-create with `make assets`. Theme: arcade fiction in
the chunky VGA style of early-90s shooters — a Portuguese Army soldier of
the 1960s/70s defends the headquarters and the fort against a fictional
foreign invasion force. The style is borrowed, every pixel is ours: nothing
is copied or traced from Wolfenstein 3D or any other game. No Nazi symbols;
the enemies are adult combatants only, no real people, no civilians or
prisoners as targets; the Mocidade Portuguesa appears only as décor.

## Music: requested tunes and their copyright status

Portugal/EU: life of the composer + 70 years, so a melody is free only when
its composer died before 1 January 1956. Only public-domain melodies are
reproduced in the game; the others got an original march instead.
